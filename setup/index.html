📋 Where ratings go

This page works out the field ids from a Google Form and prints the block to paste into app/utils/config.js. Nothing here changes the live app on its own.
Currently wired: yes

1 · Build the form

Five questions, all Short answer, named exactly: Plant, Name, Rating, Category, From.
⚠ In Settings, leave “Collect email addresses” off and mark nothing Required — a required question makes every submission fail silently, and we would not find out until after the event.

2 · Get the pre-filled link

In the form's ⋮ menu choose Get pre-filled link. Type the matching word into each box — the word Plant into Plant, Rating into Rating, and so on — then Get link → Copy link.
Not the Send → link, and not the address bar: those carry no field ids at all.
