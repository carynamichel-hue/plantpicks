# New Plant Selections — rate & take notes

A follow-along for Overdevest Nurseries' new-plant presentation. Every plant in
the talk, numbered as it is in the deck: rate it out of five, keep your own
notes, and send yourself a copy at the end.

**Open it here: https://carynamichel-hue.github.io/plantpicks/**

There is no account and nothing to install. **Your notes stay on your phone** —
they are never sent anywhere, and the only way they leave the device is you
sending them to yourself. Star ratings go to Overdevest so they know what people
liked, and there is a switch to turn that off.

Once opened it works with no signal, so a weak connection in the greenhouse
does not stop you following along.

This repository holds the **built site**.

Built with Nuxt at Overdevest Nurseries.
